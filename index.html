The brief

Your brief, as we understood it.

You want to build buzz on X while the event is running, and come away with creative you can use in your marketing for years. X is the priority, with LinkedIn a distant second. Success is 250K impressions across everything we post, and 10 new applications in the week after from people who saw the event on X.

You asked what you might be missing

The reach will come from the people in the room.

On X, a typical original post from @youderian gets around 1–3K views, and @ecomfuelco's videos have done 500–1,000. Reaching 250K across everything we post would take well over a hundred of Andrew's posts, so ECF's own channels won't get there alone. The rest has to come from the roughly 300 owners at the event, and especially the handful with real audiences, posting and quote-posting while it's happening. That's where we'd put the extra effort.

Amplify

A plan for the guest list

Before we land, we'd go through your list with you, pick the 15–20 attendees who have audiences and agree a moment with each of them.

Proof

Member conversations

4 mic'd one-on-one conversations with owners, filmed casually around the event, like the ones Camp Mondays pulled off. They talk about where they were stuck and what changed. It's the kind of proof your apply page runs on, in video form.
